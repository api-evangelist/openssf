Ericsson Software Technology successfully met the stringent obligations of the EU Cyber Resilience Act (CRA) by fundamentally shifting to upstream collaboration. Guided by OpenSSF principles, they eliminated private forks and contributed over 1,400 dependency updates and security fixes directly to open source communities.
