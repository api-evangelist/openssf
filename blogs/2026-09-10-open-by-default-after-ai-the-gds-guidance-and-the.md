September 2026 By Sal Kimmich and Simon John Executive Summary In early May 2026, NHS England issued a reported internal guidance note, SDLC-8, mandating the removal of public access to several hundred GitHub repositories. The stated reason was AI-accelerated vulnerability discovery. The actual effect was to contradict years of established UK government open source policy with no public consultation, no published threat model, and no evidence the closures removed any meaningful attacker advantage.
