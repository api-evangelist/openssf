In this episode of What’s in the SOSS, ActiveState CEO Abby Kearns breaks down the rapidly evolving open source security landscape. The conversation explores why reactive post-build scanning fails, the risks of AI-driven code ingestion, and how impending EU CRA mandates will impact enterprise software supply chains.
