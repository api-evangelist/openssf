The OpenSSF announced expanded membership during its Community Day Europe, welcoming four new members: A-Team Systems, Emphere, DACHS IT GMBH, and JetBrains. The Foundation also released significant CRA resources to deepen Cyber Resilience Act (CRA) preparedness. These updates come as regulatory pressure grows and mandatory CRA reporting takes effect, driving the need for clear guidance and systemic collaboration on open source security.
